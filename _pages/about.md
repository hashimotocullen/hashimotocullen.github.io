I am a PhD student at Sorbonne Université, within the [LPSM](https://www.lpsm.paris) unit. I work on forecasting time series with latent variable models, with a focus on electricity consumption prediction. I am supervised by [Sylvain le Corff](https://sylvainlc.github.io), [Benjamin Guedj](https://bguedj.github.io) and [Yannig Goude](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~yannig.goude/about.html).

I took my master's degree in Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Development at UCL in 2023-2024. Before that, I worked in financial services, supporting clients using quant tools and fixed income functionalities.

My undergraduate degree is in Mathematics from the University of Glasgow (2017-2021). I grew up between Paris and Cambridge (UK), and speak French and English natively.
