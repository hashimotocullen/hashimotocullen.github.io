I am a PhD student at Sorbonne Université, within the [LPSM](https://www.lpsm.paris) unit. I work on predicting time series with few-shot learning and probabilistic modelling methods, with a focus on electricity consumption time series. I am supervised by [Sylvain le Corff](https://sylvainlc.github.io), [Benjamin Guedj](https://bguedj.github.io) and [Yannig Goude](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~yannig.goude/about.html).
